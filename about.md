<h2>About</h2>

<p>
I am Omid Mostafaei Nia, also formally known as Hossein Mostafaei Nia. I work at the intersection of business analytics, marketplace operations, industrial engineering, and decision-support systems.
</p>

<p>
My main focus is turning operational complexity into measurable, explainable, and actionable analytics. I have worked on vendor performance systems, KPI governance, Tableau dashboards, incident detection logic, reward and incentive frameworks, and marketplace decision-support workflows.
</p>

This portfolio brings together selected dashboards, marketplace analytics projects, academic research, and data science work. The public dashboard versions use anonymized or synthetic datasets to preserve the analytical structure of the original work without exposing confidential company data.

<h3>Academic Background</h3>

<p>
I hold a Master’s degree in Sustainable Industrial Engineering – Operations Management from Grenoble INP – Université Grenoble Alpes, where my research internship focused on ethical risks in Industry 4.0 and the integration of ethical considerations into Manufacturing System Design.
</p>

<p>
I also hold a Master’s degree in Industrial Management – Operations Research from Kharazmi University. My thesis focused on stock market prediction using machine learning and deep learning approaches, including LSTM, 1D CNN, candlestick chart image classification, and technical-indicator-based image conversion.
</p>

<p>
My Bachelor’s degree was in Textile Engineering – Textile Chemistry and Fiber Science from Amirkabir University of Technology, where I worked on modelling synthetic thread as a microfluidic device.
