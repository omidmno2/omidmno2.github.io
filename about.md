<h3>Hello, I’m Omid.</h3>

<p>
I am a Senior Business Data Analyst based in Grenoble, France, with 5+ years of experience in operations, product, and performance analytics for digital platforms. My work focuses on turning complex marketplace operations into clear KPI systems, dashboards, decision workflows, and data-driven interventions.
</p>

<p>
I work across the full analytics cycle: defining metrics, building data pipelines, designing dashboards, analyzing operational behavior, detecting anomalies, and translating insights into product, marketing, finance, and operations decisions.
</p>

<h3>What I Work On</h3>

<p>
At SnappFood, an online food delivery platform, I have worked on vendor performance analytics for a large-scale marketplace. This includes KPI framework design, vendor segmentation, operational dashboards, performance scoring, reward and cost logic, E-CRM analysis, and incident-detection logic to identify platform-driven KPI shocks.
</p>

<p>
A major part of my work has been connecting analytics with real operational decisions: identifying underperforming cohorts, measuring the impact of interventions, supporting scalable reporting workflows, and helping teams make fairer and more consistent performance decisions.
</p>

<p>
Alongside my industry work, I also conducted research at G-SCOP Laboratory and LISTIC on digital technologies, ethical risks, and decision checkpoints in industrial systems. This research strengthened my interest in responsible data systems, human-in-the-loop decision-making, and the governance of analytics in operational environments.
</p>
