## Overview

This project focused on designing and scaling a vendor performance governance framework for a large online food delivery marketplace.

The framework connected vendor performance KPIs with recurring business workflows, including performance evaluation, positive reinforcement, operational follow-up, stakeholder reporting, and downstream decision support.

The goal was to move vendor performance management from fragmented monthly handling toward a more structured, measurable, and repeatable process.

This public version intentionally describes the project at a high level. Exact thresholds, financial rules, eligibility logic, vendor segmentation rules, communication templates, internal systems, and operational procedures are not disclosed.

---

## Business Context

Marketplace quality depends heavily on vendor reliability.

When vendors repeatedly create operational friction, the effect is not limited to one order. It can affect customer experience, delivery reliability, operational workload, marketplace trust, and long-term vendor quality.

At the same time, a fair performance system should not only focus on corrective action. It should also recognize vendors that maintain strong operational performance.

The business need was to create a governance framework that could support both sides:

- identifying vendors that require operational attention;
- recognizing vendors with consistently strong performance;
- creating repeatable monthly performance workflows;
- improving consistency in how vendor performance outcomes are handled;
- connecting KPI logic with business action.

---

## Problem

Before the framework became more structured, several parts of vendor performance management were manual, fragmented, or difficult to scale.

The main challenges included:

- limited consistency in recurring performance workflows;
- manual preparation of monthly outputs;
- difficulty tracking vendor eligibility and performance outcomes over time;
- the need to connect KPI logic with stakeholder workflows;
- the need to balance corrective mechanisms with positive incentives;
- the risk of inconsistent vendor treatment if rules, timing, or exceptional cases were not clearly managed.

The project addressed these challenges by creating a more structured analytical and operational layer for performance governance.

---

## Solution

The solution was a vendor performance framework built around KPI-based logic and recurring workflow design.

At a high level, the framework supported:

- defining the eligible vendor population;

- identifying vendors requiring follow-up;
- identifying vendors eligible for positive recognition;
- preparing recurring stakeholder outputs;
- maintaining historical tracking;
- supporting vendor-facing performance visibility;

- identifying opportunities for automation.

## Framework Design

The framework was designed around a monthly performance cycle.

At a high level, the cycle included:

1. preparing the relevant vendor performance data;
2. validating the data and business rules;
3. applying KPI-based evaluation logic;
4. separating vendors into different performance outcome groups;
5. preparing outputs for stakeholder review;
6. supporting follow-up, communication, or downstream action;
7. archiving results for historical tracking and governance.

The public version does not disclose the internal rules used in any of these steps.

## Analytical Logic

The framework was based on a set of operational performance indicators.

The exact internal thresholds and business rules are not included in this public version, but the logic followed a general structure:

1. define eligible vendor population;
2. calculate vendor-level performance KPIs for the period;
3. compare each vendor’s performance against acceptable thresholds;
4. identify vendors eligible for rewards;
5. identify vendors exceeding operational limits;
6. generate outputs for reporting, follow-up, and downstream workflows;
7. archive monthly results for historical tracking.

The logic was designed to support both fairness and scalability.

## Key Components

### Vendor Eligibility Logic

The framework required a clear definition of which vendors should be included in the program.

This included rules related to vendor lifecycle, operational activity, category, and program scope. The purpose was to make sure the framework applied consistently and did not mix vendors that were not comparable.

### KPI Calculation Layer

The project translated operational events into vendor-level KPIs.

These KPIs were used to evaluate whether a vendor stayed within acceptable performance limits or qualified for positive recognition.

### Cost Calculation Workflow

For vendors exceeding defined operational limits, the framework generated structured monthly outputs for downstream processing.

This required careful validation because the output was connected to sensitive financial and vendor-facing workflows.

### Reward Workflow

For vendors with strong performance, the framework generated reward eligibility outputs.

The reward side was important because the project was not only about corrective action. It also aimed to create positive reinforcement for vendors maintaining strong operational quality.

### Vendor Communication

The project included vendor-facing communication flows to explain performance outcomes, encourage improvement, and increase awareness of the performance framework.

In the public version, internal message templates, communication links, and operational details are intentionally excluded.

### Reporting and Governance

The monthly outputs were shared with relevant internal stakeholders to support monitoring, financial alignment, operational follow-up, and program governance.
