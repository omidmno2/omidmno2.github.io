## Overview

This page summarizes selected marketplace analytics initiatives I worked on across vendor performance, operations, product requirements, business analysis, and decision-support workflows.

The purpose of this page is not to document every internal project in detail. Instead, it provides a high-level view of the range of analytical and cross-functional work I contributed to in a large-scale food delivery marketplace environment.

This public version intentionally excludes confidential company information, internal documents, stakeholder names, exact business rules, financial details, internal links, implementation logic, and sensitive operational workflows.

---

## Why This Page Exists

Not every project needs a separate portfolio page.

Some initiatives were large enough to deserve their own case study, such as vendor performance governance, incident detection, or excellence badge logic. Other initiatives were smaller, exploratory, internal, or connected to broader programs.

This page groups those additional initiatives into themes so the broader scope of my work is visible without turning the portfolio into an internal archive.

A significant part of my work focused on vendor performance systems and the governance around them.

These initiatives included work related to:

- performance-based communication;
- operational follow-up;

- performance-related stakeholder reporting.

The common goal was to make vendor performance evaluation more structured, measurable, and actionable.

- vendor consultation support;
- performance diagnosis dashboards;

## 3. Reward, Incentive & Vendor Motivation Models

Another group of initiatives focused on designing better incentive structures for vendors.

The main analytical challenge was to connect vendor performance, vendor maturity, business goals, and reward eligibility into a more structured framework.

The work included:

- performance-based reward concepts;
- tiered incentive logic;
- vendor lifecycle thinking;
- reward basket analysis;
- vendor engagement mechanisms;
- benchmarking of global marketplace incentive models;
- alignment between vendor motivation and customer experience.

The goal was to move beyond isolated rewards and create a more coherent incentive logic linked to performance and vendor development.

## 5. Marketplace Growth & Supply Initiatives

Some initiatives focused on marketplace growth, vendor activation, availability, order generation, and supply-side performance.

These projects explored questions such as:

- how to improve vendor engagement;
- how to support new vendors;
- how to increase availability;
- how to diagnose low-order or no-order vendors;
- how to improve supply quality in specific operational windows;
- how to evaluate commercial actions through performance indicators;
- how to connect vendor behavior with order outcomes.

The common theme was using data to understand where marketplace supply was weak and how targeted interventions could improve performance.

## 6. Commercial & Operational Reporting

Several initiatives involved recurring or campaign-based reporting for commercial and operational teams.

These reports were designed to help teams monitor progress, evaluate initiatives, and communicate results.

The work included:

- mid-cycle and final reports;
- monthly performance reviews;
- initiative tracking;
- KPI packs;
- performance battle reports;
- retention and engagement reporting;
- operational improvement summaries;
- decision-support presentations.

The goal was to turn operational activity into measurable business learning.

## 7. Customer Experience & Service Quality Analysis

Some initiatives focused on the connection between vendor behavior and customer experience.

These projects explored service quality, customer dissatisfaction, order issues, product availability, and operational friction.

The work included:

- service quality analysis;
- customer complaint or comment interpretation;
- performance issue diagnosis;
- vendor-side friction analysis;
- support and communication material;
- customer-facing impact assessment.

## 8. Benchmarking & Strategic Research

Benchmarking was also an important part of several initiatives.

I worked on comparisons with other marketplace and food delivery platforms to understand how similar companies approach:

- vendor badges;
- reward systems;
- vendor onboarding;
- performance incentives;
- service quality programs;
- customer-facing quality signals;
- commercial growth tactics;
- operational performance management.

Benchmarking helped frame internal decisions and avoid designing programs only from an internal perspective.

## My Role Across These Initiatives

Across these initiatives, my role typically involved a combination of analytics, business design, documentation, and stakeholder support.

- designing dashboards or reporting outputs;

- preparing BRDs, PRDs, proposals, or presentation materials;
- reviewing performance outcomes;
- identifying process gaps;
- supporting cross-functional alignment;
- documenting workflows for continuity.

The common thread was connecting data with operational and business decisions.
